# FUTURE_DS_01
 Sales Performance Analysis – Task 1
 
i)Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze business sales data to identify key trends, top-performing products, and regional performance, and provide actionable business insights.

ii)Tools Used:
Power BI
Microsoft Excel

iii)Dataset
Superstore Sales Dataset (Kaggle)

iv) Key Insights
Sales show a steady trend over time, indicating consistent business performance.
The Technology category generates the highest revenue among all categories.
The West region is the top-performing region in terms of sales.
A small number of products contribute significantly to total revenue.
Some regions show high sales but relatively lower profit, indicating pricing inefficiencies.

v)Recommendations
Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products to maximize revenue.
Increase inventory for high-demand categories like Technology.
Review pricing strategies in low-profit regions to improve profit margins.
Reduce excessive discounts to maintain profitability.

vi)Dashboard
Power BI dashboard included in this repository
